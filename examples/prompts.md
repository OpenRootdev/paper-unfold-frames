# Prompts that worked

Tested with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, 2048×2048 (returned as a 1024² grid). The resulting grids are on the `assets` branch so this branch stays small.

## White sheet, sketch already on it

Result: [grid-sketch-white.png](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ker102/paper-unfold-frames/assets/examples/grid-sketch-white.png)

A 3x3 grid of nine stop-motion animation frames, photographed straight top-down, of the SAME single sheet of white paper being unfolded, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom. The paper has a hand-drawn black fine-liner sketch on it: two short lines of neat handwriting at the top reading "every draft" and "gets a second look", and below them a simple line drawing of two mountain peaks with hatched shading. Frame 1 a tight, fully closed crumpled paper ball with only tiny fragments of black ink lines visible in the creases; frame 2 the ball loosening, a few more ink fragments showing; frame 3 the ball cracking open at the top; frame 4 half open like a crumpled flower, parts of the handwriting and mountain visible but distorted by folds; frame 5 more open with big wrinkled petals; frame 6 mostly open, heavily wrinkled, sketch mostly readable; frame 7 almost flat with deep crease lines through the sketch; frame 8 flat wrinkled sheet with irregular torn ripped edges, the full handwriting and mountain sketch clearly readable; frame 9 the same flat sheet lying still. The ink always stays on the paper and follows its creases. Each frame on an identical solid flat pure chroma blue (#0047FF) background with no texture, the paper centered in its cell, same soft daylight from the upper left, realistic crease shading, no shadows on the background, no hands, no borders, no grid lines between cells.

## Lined notebook page (writing added in code)

Result: [grid-notebook.png](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ker102/paper-unfold-frames/assets/examples/grid-notebook.png)

A 3x3 grid of nine stop-motion animation frames, photographed straight top-down, of the SAME single lined notebook page (cream paper, faint blue horizontal ruled lines, one red margin line, ragged spiral-torn left edge) being unfolded, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom: frame 1 a small, tight, fully closed crumpled paper wad, slightly flattened and oval; frame 2 the wad loosening; frame 3 one corner peeling open; frame 4 half open, folded flaps sticking up; frame 5 more open with large wrinkled flaps; frame 6 mostly open, heavily wrinkled; frame 7 almost flat with deep creases; frame 8 flat wrinkled lined page, rectangular portrait shape with torn left edge; frame 9 the same flat wrinkled page lying still. The page is blank apart from the ruled lines, no handwriting. Each frame on an identical solid flat pure chroma blue (#0047FF) background with no texture, the paper centered in its cell, same soft daylight from the upper left, realistic crease shading, no shadows on the background, no hands, no text, no borders, no grid lines between cells.
